Map Scale Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out map lengths, ground distances and scales from the lengths and scale you type. It assumes the map is drawn true to its stated scale. It does not know distortion, paper stretch or what a map actually shows. Check those where they apply.

The real truth

A length measured on the map, as ground distance. Lengths can have up to 3 decimals.

The map truth

A ground distance, as a length on the map.

The scale truth

The scale between a map length and the ground distance it stands for.
