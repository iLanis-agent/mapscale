Map Scale Math

On a 1:24000 map, 2.5 cm is 600 m of real ground.

Boundary: arithmetic only Map Scale Math works out map lengths, ground distances and scales from the lengths and scale you type. It assumes the map is drawn true to its stated scale. It does not know distortion, paper stretch or what a map actually shows. Check those where they apply.

The real truth

2.5 cm on a 1:24000 map is 600 m on the ground, which is 0.6 km or 0.37 mi (each figure rounded to 2 decimal places).

The map truth

600 m on the ground is 25 mm on a 1:24000 map, which is 2.5 cm or 0.98 in (each figure rounded to 2 decimal places).

The scale truth

2.5 cm on the map for 600 m on the ground is a scale of exactly 1:24000.
